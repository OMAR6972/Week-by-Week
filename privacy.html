Privacy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Week by Week is a student-run course website for an engineering class. It shows each week's material, deadlines, exams and announcements. It is not run by the university. This page says, in plain words, what the site keeps about you.

You don't need an account

Everything on the site works without signing in. An account only adds extras: your settings following you between devices, and notifications by email or on your phone.

If you make an account or sign in with Google

  • Your email address and the name you chose (or the name on your Google account). That is all we receive from Google: your name and email address. We never see your Google password, your contacts, your files or anything else in your Google account.
  • Your settings: which subjects you take, which notifications you want, your Home layout, the GPA numbers you typed into the GPA calculator, and similar choices, so they follow you to another device.
  • Your phone's push address, only if you turn on push notifications, so the site can send notifications to that phone.

If you don't sign in

Your choices are stored only in your own browser, on your own device. They never reach our database.

Usage counts

The site counts which pages and materials get opened, using a random number stored in your browser that isn't linked to your name or email. This tells the site's admins which things are useful. Signed-in students also see their own activity on their Home page.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase stores the site's data and accounts.
  • Brevo delivers the notification emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.
  • Google handles the "Continue with Google" sign-in.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Your choices

  • You can stop any notification, or all of them, from the account menu.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can ask for your account and everything stored with it to be deleted: email electric.junior23.27@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
